AI-generated marketing creates a new operational problem: teams can draft faster than they can verify. For technical founders, the right answer is not slower writing. It is a validation gate that catches factual, numeric, and source-backed claims before they become public copy.

FreshProof is the pattern: treat every meaningful assertion as something that can be checked, routed, and either supported or sent back for review. That turns publishing from a confidence game into an editorial system.

For Corvo Labs, the useful workflow is simple: capture the idea, generate a draft, extract claims, validate the risky ones, then open a GitHub PR for final review and publication. The PR matters because it preserves authorship, review, and rollback in the same place engineering teams already work.
